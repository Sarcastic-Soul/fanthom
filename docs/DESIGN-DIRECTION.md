# Design direction

## Who and where

Team leads, sales reps and engineers on a laptop at their desk, the day after a long call they
missed or half-listened to. They want the outcome of an 8-person, 1-hour meeting in two minutes
without pressing play, and then jump to the exact moment when something needs checking.

## Feel

A tool, not a toy. Dense, calm and precise, like Linear or a good audio editor. The one
signature element is the **speaker timeline**: one lane per person, showing who spoke when
across the whole hour. It doubles as the scrubber. On an 8-person call it tells you more in one
glance than the video does.

## Rules for this project

- One accent color, tinted neutrals (never pure black or white).
- Speaker colors are the only other color, and they are muted, not neon.
- Phosphor icons, one weight.
- Numbers in tabular figures. Timestamps in a mono or tabular font.
- Everything that mentions a moment (summary bullet, action item, chapter, search hit) links to it.

## Must not look like

- A generic AI SaaS landing page (gradients, glow, purple, glass, sparkles).
- A shadcn demo with cards inside cards.
- A video app where the video is the hero. The transcript and the outcome are the hero.
